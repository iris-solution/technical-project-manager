# Take-Home Task — Technical Project Manager (OpAnalysts): Brief

- **Time box:** 90 minutes
- **Format:** plain text or a simple doc. No slides, no templates required.

> Our recommendation: Submit in whatever state it's in at 90 minutes. We are not looking for a refined document, we are looking for your thought process.

## Scenario

You have just been assigned to lead a project that is already in trouble.

**The project:** build an internal fraud-detection module that pulls transaction data from three different sources (two internal systems, one third-party vendor feed), flags suspicious patterns, and pushes alerts to an operations team who act on them daily.

**Status:** 6 weeks in, planned for 10 weeks total.

- The vendor feed integration is delayed; the vendor says 2 to 3 more weeks, no firm commitment.
- The two data engineers on the project disagree on the data model: one wants to rebuild it now to handle the delay cleanly, the other wants to patch around it and keep moving.
- The operations team who will use the alerts is asking for a demo in 2 weeks. The leadership team has also been asking for updates.
- You have no prior relationship with the vendor, the data engineers, or the operations team. You were added to this project yesterday.
- You have a data analyst who can build reports but has flagged she is "not sure what data is actually reliable right now."

**Request:** Produce a plan for how you would run this project from here.

## What to Produce

- Your assessment of the actual state of the project (not just a restatement of the brief; what do you think is really going on, and what's your biggest concern)
- Your plan for the next 2 weeks specifically, including what happens to the demo commitment
- How you resolve the disagreement between the two data engineers, and what you'd tell each of them
- What you escalate versus what you handle yourself, and to whom
- Anything you'd want to ask or verify before finalizing this plan, if you had more time

Do not write a full project charter or Gantt chart. We want your reasoning and your calls, not a formatted deliverable.
